How a project runs

No bloated discovery phase. Sometimes you reach out, sometimes I do, either way you'll see real work before you pay anything.

It starts one of two ways

You send over what you need, or if I've already come across your business and think I can genuinely help, I'll reach out first, research already done.

You see it before you pay

I'll walk through specific things I'd change about your site and build a working example. No payment, no commitment, just proof it's worth doing.

We scope it together

If it's a fit, I reply with a plan and a realistic timeline before anything else starts, one I can actually commit to, since I'm not splitting the week across other clients.

I build it, then you launch

Your site is the only thing on my desk until it ships, so you'll see real progress as it happens, not updates squeezed in between someone else's project. A final walkthrough, and it goes live.
