A Lawndale fixture for over 70 years

Windham Shoe Repair has been resoling, repairing and shining shoes on Hawthorne Blvd for generations. It's the kind of shop where regulars don't need to explain what they want, because the person behind the counter already knows.

Today the shop is run by Iggy, a cobbler customers describe as a legend. He's not interested in the cheapest possible fix, he's interested in the right one, work that holds up and prices that are fair for what you're getting.

Alongside shoe repair, the shop offers licensed key cutting, a holdover from an era when a neighborhood shop like this one handled whatever its customers needed.

Whether it's a quick shine before an interview or a full resole on boots you've had for a decade, Windham has been the place in Lawndale to bring it.
