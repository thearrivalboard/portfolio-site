Our story

Stoked Chocolates was created out of a passion for chocolate and surfing. Each box is inspired by the stoke of a day spent in the ocean, surrounded by friends and family, and made by hand with the finest ingredients and paper. We hope you love them as much as we do, and spread the stoke.
