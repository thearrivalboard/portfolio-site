Serving the South Bay since 1982

Phanny's has been a neighborhood fixture for over 40 years, specializing in breakfast, lunch and coffee. What started as a single counter in Redondo Beach grew into a second location in El Segundo, but the approach never changed: fresh ingredients, no shortcuts, and a burrito worth coming back for.

We're proud to be the kind of place where regulars know the order before they reach the counter, and where a health score of A and over a thousand combined reviews says more than we ever could.

Whether you're grabbing a burrito before work or settling in with a cup of Stumptown coffee, Phanny's is still, at its core, a simple neighborhood breakfast counter.
