About

I'm Keaton Marr. I build websites that convert.

You talk to the person actually building your site, start to finish.

I'm Keaton Marr, a 16-year-old student and entrepreneur. I've been trying to build a successful business since middle school, trading, crypto, a clothing brand, bike repair and resale, a cologne sample business, skateboards, dropshipping, you name it. Web design is the one that's actually stuck. I've been doing it for about 2 years now, with one real goal: prove that a successful business is possible at a young age. When I'm not working on a site, I'm probably rock climbing or playing volleyball.

My parents work in marketing, and they're where I learned most of what I know about running a business. Since then I've built plenty of high-converting ecommerce and dropshipping sites, and gotten the chance to build for, and interview, a few self-made millionaires along the way, conversations that taught me more about business than any class could.

I build with modern tools, including AI-assisted coding, and whichever platform actually fits the job: fully custom code, Shopify, Wix, or Webflow. That means faster builds without sacrificing how the site looks or performs.

I only take on one project at a time. No juggling five clients across three time zones, no context-switching between your site and someone else's. While I'm building yours, it's the only thing on my desk, which is how a full site comes together in days instead of months. You'll hear from me throughout, see real progress as it happens, and never have to wonder where your project landed in a queue.

I also don't just wait around for people to reach out. If I come across a business whose site I think I could genuinely improve, I'll research it myself, put together specific things I'd change, and build a working example before ever asking for payment. You get to see exactly what I'd do before you decide anything.

I'm doing this while I still have the time, before graduating and moving on to whatever's next. If you run a local business, a marketing or social media brand, a production studio, or anything that depends on people trusting you enough to reach out, that's exactly the kind of project I want to hear about.
