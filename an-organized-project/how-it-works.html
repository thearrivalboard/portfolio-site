How it works

A clear, thoughtful process, designed to support real life.

Intentional, efficient and tailored to you. Whether you're preparing for a move or seeking sustainable systems, we meet you where you are and guide you forward, step by step.

01

Start the conversation

We begin with a brief phone call or email to understand your overall goals, timeline and immediate concerns. This helps us determine the best next step and ensures our approach is aligned from the start.

02

Consultation, in person or virtual

A one-time working session with a Certified Professional Organizer®, completed before any hands-on organizing begins.

  • Clarify your goals and desired outcomes
  • Define the project scope and estimate timelines
  • Discuss customized organizing strategies
  • Suggest products and trusted resources
  • Take measurements, notes and photos on-site
  • Answer questions and talk through what's challenging
03

Organizing sessions

Once you're ready, we schedule your organizing session and confirm additional days as needed. Sessions are customized to your project and may include one or more organizers depending on scope.

  • Hands-on editing and decision support
  • Sorting, categorizing and containerizing
  • Labeling and system design
  • Paper management and shredding coordination
  • Donation, consignment and discard coordination
  • Maintenance recommendations for long-term success
