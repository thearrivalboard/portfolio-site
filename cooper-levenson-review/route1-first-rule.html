1970s

Gaming law didn't exist yet. So we helped write it.

Cooper Levenson opened in Atlantic City in 1957 with two attorneys. In the 1970s, the firm helped draft the legislation that allowed casino gaming in Atlantic City, and its lawyers have worked inside that regulatory system ever since.

That history is why operators, suppliers and casino owners call us when a new market opens. We know how the rules were built, and how they change.

Frank Fahrenkopf, former head of the American Gaming Association, has credited Cooper Levenson with leading the genesis of gaming law as a field.
