Visit the Shop

Hello, we're Laurence and Sally

For 27 years, our El Segundo shop has been known as the source for antiques, collectibles and furniture at fantastic prices. What started as a shared love of hunting for unusual, beautiful and rare things turned into a shop people drive across LA to visit.

You might have seen us on A&E's "Storage Wars," or on the international show "Baggage Battles." We've been selling online for 14 years too, and have been members of eBay since 1998, so you can search, browse and buy from us anytime, not just when you can make it into the shop.

We're also longtime participants in Record Store Day, and our collection of rare and collectible vinyl is one of the reasons people come back again and again.

Stop by often, we add new items from our vast inventory daily.
