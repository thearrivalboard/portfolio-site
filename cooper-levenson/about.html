About the firm

Attorneys with decades of legal experience.

Founded in 1957, Cooper Levenson is a full-service firm serving a client base that spans the region and the globe, from individuals to corporations to sovereign governments.

Our story

Nearly seventy years, one firm.

  1. 1957Cooper Levenson is founded with a commitment to the community that still shapes the firm today.
  2. Casino gambling comes to Atlantic CityThe firm represents every department of a casino, from security and construction to facilities and personnel, from day one.
  3. 1970sCooper Levenson helps originate gaming law as a field of practice.
  4. TodayA full-service firm across six offices in New Jersey, Delaware, Pennsylvania, Nevada, Florida and New York, with 27 practice areas and a nationally recognized gaming law practice.
What we stand for

Community, diversity, and the next generation of lawyers.

Community service

Founded with a deep sense of commitment to the community, attorneys and staff are encouraged to make a difference, from national leadership roles to volunteering in neighborhood settings.

Diversity

We believe our future rests in our collective ability to embrace change and encourage diversity through our leadership, employment, and continued community support.

An eight-week paid program in our Atlantic City office for qualified law students, with assignments across practice areas and direct access to the firm's attorneys.
