What we handle

Six ways we work with operators.

Our clients range from sovereign governments to casino hotel resorts to the vendors that serve the industry. We assist through licensing and the going live process, and stay with clients as legislation and the market evolve.

Market entry & licensing

Applications, key employee licensing and software or equipment approval for operators entering a new state.

Suppliers & vendors

Registration and approval for platform providers, payment processors and equipment manufacturers.

Compliance & AML

Transactions & change of control

Casino defense
