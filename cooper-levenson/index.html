Gaming & iGaming law

Since the first casino opened on the boardwalk, we've been in the room.

Our Gaming and iGaming practice advises sports wagering, esports, interactive casino and online gaming operators, from licensing and compliance through M&A and corporate structuring. Clients range from sovereign governments to casino resorts to the vendors that serve them.
