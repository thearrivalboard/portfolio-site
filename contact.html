A few things people ask

How much does a website cost?

It depends entirely on what you need. Send over the details and I'll reply with a quote tailored to the project. No generic pricing tiers.

How fast can you actually turn it around?

Most projects land somewhere between 1 and 2 weeks once scope is locked in. I'm not optimizing for the fastest possible turnaround, I'm optimizing for getting it right, and bigger builds with custom features take longer. I'll give you a real timeframe up front.

Do you work with multiple clients at the same time?

No. I take one project at a time, start to finish, before starting the next. It means a short wait if I'm already booked, but once your project starts, it has my full attention until it's live, not a few hours a week squeezed between other work.

Do I have to reach out first?

No. Sometimes I reach out first. If I come across a business I think I can genuinely help, I'll research your current site, put together specific things I'd change, and build you a working example, before asking for a cent. If you like what you see, we talk. If not, no hard feelings.

Do I need to have my content ready?

It helps but isn't required to get started. Send what you have and we'll fill in the gaps together.

What if I just want to talk it through first?

That's exactly what the form above is for. No commitment, just a starting point for a conversation.
