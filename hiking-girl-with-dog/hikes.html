Upcoming hikes

Our group hikes are scheduled monthly with a size limit so everyone stays together. All dogs are welcome as long as they meet our Pup Guidelines.

Reserve early, space is limited. Full refunds are available up to 4 days before the event, no questions asked.

Some trailheads require a parking pass, an Adventure Pass ($5 day / $30 annual) or an America the Beautiful Pass. Not every hike needs one, we'll note it in your registration confirmation.
