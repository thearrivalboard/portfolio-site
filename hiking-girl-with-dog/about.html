Hello, I'm Jamie

I'm a holistic hiking coach, the founder of Hiking Girl with Dog, and a passionate advocate for reconnecting with nature. For over a decade, I've explored dog-friendly trails across LA and the South Bay, sharing those adventures to inspire others along the way.

What began in 2014 as simple weekend escapes grew into something much deeper, a path to healing, confidence and community. Along the way I discovered just how empowering hiking can be, for both people and pups, especially for those navigating life transitions, burnout, or simply craving more connection to themselves and the natural world.

That's what inspired me to turn this passion into a heart-driven business, helping others experience the same transformation through personalized coaching and curated group hikes. I specialize in small guided hikes and private sessions designed to meet you where you are, whether you're seasoned or just starting out, and I take the guesswork out of finding trails that are truly dog-friendly.

My mission is simple: to create meaningful, welcoming hiking experiences that leave you feeling more grounded, empowered and uplifted, because the trail doesn't just take you somewhere, it brings you back to yourself.

Xena

Trail Name: Pink Goat · 2012–2024

Xena was a gentle soul wrapped in 31 pounds of pure love, a mixed breed with a wise gaze and calm presence who touched countless lives during her 12 years on earth. People often said, "I wasn't a dog person, until I met Xena."

Whether on private hikes or guiding alongside me, we were true partners, always in step, always in sync. She had a gift for sensing who needed the most support, often slowing her pace to offer comfort or quietly making sure no one fell behind.

Though she crossed over the rainbow bridge in 2024, Xena still hikes with us in spirit. Her legacy lives on in the paw prints she left on the many hearts she helped open along the way.
