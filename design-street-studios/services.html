Nine ways to bring balloons into your event.

From a single arch over an entryway to a fully decorated room, each style can scale up or down to fit your event and budget.

Arches

Our most requested piece. A balloon arch frames an entrance, a photo spot or a stage, and can be built organic and asymmetrical or classic and symmetrical, in any color palette.

Columns & Party Standards

Tall, freestanding and easy to place in pairs along an aisle, a buffet table or an entrance. A simple way to add height and color without a full installation.

Garlands, Cascades & Demi-Arches

Organic, flowing balloon garlands that drape over a table, cascade down a wall or wrap a backdrop. We mix in faux florals and greenery when it fits the look.

Photo Ops, Marquees, Walls & Backdrops

A dedicated spot built for photos, whether that's a full balloon wall, a marquee letter setup or a themed backdrop built around your event.

Numbers, Names & Words

Mosaic-filled numbers, names and short words, built to stand on their own or anchor a bigger installation.

Centerpieces

Table-ready pieces sized to leave room for place settings, matched to your event's colors and theme.

Bouquets

A simple, affordable way to add balloons to a smaller event or gift, ready to grab and go.

Drops & Ceiling

Balloons held overhead and released on cue, or a full ceiling treatment that transforms a room the moment guests walk in.

Big Balloons

Oversized balloons used as a statement piece on their own, or worked into a larger installation for extra scale.

Not sure what fits your event?

Tell us what you're planning and we'll recommend a style and build a quote around it.
