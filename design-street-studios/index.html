Balloons, done responsibly

We never release balloons

We follow the California Balloon Law on every job: foil balloons are weighed and never released, and we offer a "release & tether" option for anyone who wants the look without the litter.

We reuse what we can

Bases, poles, frames and weights get reused and repurposed from job to job, so less ends up in a landfill after your event is over.
