Welcome to Design Street Studios

When balloons first came into this business over 25 years ago, it turned into a lot more than expected. That early creativity opened doors, built friendships across the industry, and led to being named 2009 Designer of the Year.

This year marks 26 years of bringing balloons to LA's Westside, from the beaches to the studios, from schools and businesses to private events in your own backyard. Wherever there's a celebration, it's time for balloons.

No matter the age, balloons bring wonder and smiles to people everywhere.

Balloons, done responsibly

We follow the California Balloon Law

Every foil balloon we use is weighed and never released. We offer a "release & tether" option for anyone who wants that look without any balloons ending up as litter.

We reuse and repurpose

Bases, poles, frames and weights get reused across jobs whenever we can, to keep waste out of the design process as much as possible.

A small team, built for big events

Design Street Studios is still a close, hands-on team. That's what lets us handle a corporate activation and a backyard birthday with the same level of care, whether it's your first event with us or your fifteenth.

Let's bring the party

Tell us about your event and we'll put together a design and quote.
