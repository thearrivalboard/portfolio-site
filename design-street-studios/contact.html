How booking works

Tell us the details

Event date, venue, type of event, theme and colors, indoors or outdoors. The more detail, the more accurate your quote.

Reserve your date

A non-refundable retainer holds your event date and applies toward your final invoice. Your date isn't held until it's paid.

We handle setup

Delivery, installation and tax are additional and included in your final quote, so there are no surprises on event day.

Worth knowing

Balloons are a choking hazard to children, we always recommend supervision and prompt cleanup of any popped pieces.

We follow the California Balloon Law on every job: foil balloons are weighed and never released into the air. If you love the look of a release, ask about our "release & tether" option instead.
