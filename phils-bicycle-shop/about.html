76 years on Hawthorne Blvd

Phil's Bicycle Shop opened its doors in 1950, and it's been fixing and selling bikes at the same Hawthorne location ever since. Today, David and his father keep that tradition going, the same honest, no-pressure service this neighborhood has relied on for three generations.

There's no sales pitch here, just straightforward answers and work that's done right. Customers mention the same thing again and again: this is a shop that looks out for you, not just the sale.

Whether you need a bike fixed, a used bike at a fair price, or something new off the wall, Phil's has been the place in Hawthorne to go since before most of the neighborhood's current residents were born.
