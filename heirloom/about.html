About

A portrait photographer and a school parent.

Nathan Gurvitch spent 15 years photographing for museums, fashion and jewelry brands, and athletes. Becoming a dad changed what mattered most to him behind the camera.

He started with picture day at his daughter's dance school. Serving on a School Site Council, he saw how hard schools work for funding, and how rarely picture day companies share what they earn. Heirloom Photos LA was built to change that.

Beautiful, lasting portraits for families, and a fair share for the schools that make them possible.
