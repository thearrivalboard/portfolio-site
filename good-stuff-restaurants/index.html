It started with a busboy's big dream

In 1979, Cris Bennett opened a burger joint in West LA with his father. It was an instant hit, so he expanded to Hermosa Beach, then Redondo Beach, El Segundo and Palos Verdes. The menu has grown, but the values haven't: hospitality, tasty food and healthy options.
