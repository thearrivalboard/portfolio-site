It began with a busboy's big dream

Cris Bennett was a busboy with big dreams. In 1979, he opened a burger joint in West LA with his father, making those dreams a reality. It was an instant hit, so he expanded to Hermosa Beach, his second success, and over the years added Redondo Beach, El Segundo and Palos Verdes.

The core values have stayed the same through all of it: hospitality, tasty food and healthy options. You are what you eat, so eat Good Stuff.

Our flagship location has been on the Strand in Hermosa Beach for 44 years, perfectly positioned to take in the beach, the pier and the volleyball courts.
